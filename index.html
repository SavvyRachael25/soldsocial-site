No. 07

Fair questions.

Will it actually sound like me?

Yes, because we do not guess. You hand over two or three things you actually wrote, texts and emails included, or you record a two minute voice memo and we transcribe it. That becomes your Voice Print: your rhythm, your openers, your emoji habits, the words you would never use. Every post is checked against it before you ever see it, and nothing goes out without your approval. Your Voice Print is yours alone; it is never shared with or compared to another agent.

Do I have to be on camera?

No. The system is built around designed posts, your listings, and your market's numbers. If you love being on camera, great, we will schedule that too. If you would rather never film a talking head in your car, you never have to.

What do you need from me?

About an hour up front for the profile conversation, your headshot and logo, and your listing links when you have them. After that, a few minutes a week to approve the queue.

Who is actually behind this?

The Savvy Digital Co., a boutique digital studio. Real people, a real strategist reviewing your content, and an engine we built ourselves. Not a faceless app with a support ticket queue.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Month to month, no contract. Your accounts are yours, your content stays yours.
